## Oryx

Observed in OryxFullD2 and OryxStanding: immense tattered bat-like wings; dark articulated Hive body, wide horizontal facial/crown structure, long central reddish face and three luminous eyes; large talons; layered mineral/chitin armor and green light in the lower chest. Wing membranes and asymmetrical tears are principal silhouette landmarks. The pinned biography distinguishes the giant ascendant raid form from campaign combat.

Authored reconstruction: physical scale, voxel layering, membrane tessellation, a ritual chest light, optional carried sword, bounded hovering gesture cycles. Threshold architecture uses a central approach, separated ritual plates, Hive ribs and a void beyond the deck. No claim of exact canonical dimensions, mechanics or a faithful map. The sword does not create or claim a second Atlas form.

## Rhulk

Observed in RhulkInfobox and Bungie concept artwork Rhulk Art1: exceptionally tall, slender red/dark anatomy; very long legs and narrow abdomen; high elongated dark head with six pale eyes; a flared sculptural collar and rib-like chest seams. The biography identifies Lubrae's Ruin as his glaive and describes kicks/dashes and resonance.

Authored reconstruction: a separate lanky skeleton, segmented leg musculature and cuffs, a long glaive with bladed head, deliberate kick preparation/extension and a bounded dash. Black terraced Pyramid geometry, gold channels and a tall resonance machine establish the final platform. The weapon is an original interpretation, not the proprietary asset. No mass/collision or combat-damage simulation.

## Nezarec

Observed in NezarecInfobox and NezarecCharge: heavy red barrel torso contrasted with dark thick limbs; narrow tall horned silver/dark head with clustered purple eyes; large curved black scythe; pale/gold asymmetric shoulder growth and pointed back appendages. The pinned biography names Night Terror and discusses his mixed Light/Dark revival. Concept-art symmetry and exact eye visibility vary by view; the voxel mask is an interpretation.

Authored reconstruction: a broad segmented body with a rig distinct from Rhulk, branching back structures, a crescent scythe and crouch/lunge/slam poses. Pale organic roots cut across broken dark Pyramid terraces with separated Light/Dark seeds. The paired effects communicate an artistic signature, not a complete mechanics simulator. No cloth/fluids or extracted textures.

All three remain **partial dedicated prototypes pending visual review**. Piece count and successful tests do not constitute artistic approval. Athéon, Templar, Cropta and their existing evidence are preserved byte for byte.

## Internal inspection notes (not user approval)

Actual Oryx and Rhulk stills were inspected at front/profile/three-quarter/cinematic/arena views. Oryx's wing span, crown and three eyes read clearly; rigid fan-like membrane panels still need art review. Rhulk's elongated six-eyed mask, long legs, collar and glaive distinguish him from Oryx and Cropta; hip volume and the blade's simplified outline merit review. Choreographed contacts are not physical IK. These observations do not promote either prototype to a validated final asset.

Nezarec's real front/profile stills show the heavy red torso, asymmetric pale shoulder, horned mask, back growths and separate crescent scythe. Inspection identified eye lights behind the mask ridge. The ocular positions were corrected and all seven lights now pass a native Blender front-ray occlusion test; the initial Nezarec set was discarded from publication and rendered again. Fine full-body detail, head contrast and anatomical proportions remain review points. The narrow mask and heavy body intentionally contrast with Rhulk's tall slender anatomy.
